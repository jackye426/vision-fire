# Watch the model detections on the user-selected clips

The linked recordings are generated locally and are not included in Git. Recreate them from the source clips with `render_all_youtube_models.py` after downloading the models and source media.

The comparison recordings show **the five frame detectors on the same frames**, plus PyroNear's released temporal smoke pipeline. Video playback is 5 fps. Frame-detector inference was sampled at **1 frame per second**, and each box is held until the next sampled second. Flame boxes are orange, smoke boxes green. The temporal panel draws its companion detector's **yellow proposals**; its final verdict appears only after a full 20-frame window. All videos are silent.

These are visual reviews of public clips, not verified accuracy scores. A persistent box can sit on an unrelated object: the battery-fire clip includes D-Fire boxes on an excavator, SoulPerforms boxes on a wall/door, and a false smoke verdict from PyroNear's temporal pipeline on a yellow bollard.
